# v2 pilot — observation log

*The pilot feature (khealth) is the vehicle; this log is the deliverable. Every planner or
executor session in the pilot appends here before it ends — friction observed while fresh
beats friction reconstructed later. One line is enough; a pattern earns a paragraph.*

## What to watch (from CONTRACT.md next-step 7 and the 2026-08-30 principles session)

- **Interruptions** — every time the human had to intervene: what forced it, was it his to decide?
- **Escalation quality** — did the decisions that reached him actually have blast radius, and did
  options-first hold, or did a recommendation anchor him?
- **Brief quality** — did briefs stay outcome-only, or did process leak in? Was the Surface pinned
  well enough to write the acceptance tests?
- **Grading** — where "done" needed non-deterministic judgment: capture the concrete case so the
  deterministic-vs-judgment line gets decided on evidence (open question from 2026-08-30).
- **Guard friction** — every time the contract-integrity guard blocked or warned: correct catch or
  false positive?
- **kinfra fit** — anywhere kinfra's Python-container assumptions strained (EVOLUTIONS.md #6).
- **Adversarial test** (at feature close) — a fresh session tries to make every blocking criterion
  pass while violating the spec's intent. If it succeeds, the contract layer isn't holding.
